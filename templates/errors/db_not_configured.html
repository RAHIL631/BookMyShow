BookMySeat is connected to your database, but it needs to create the Tables (Migrations) to show movie data.

2. Trigger Build: Go to Deployments -> Click your latest deployment -> Click "Redeploy".
3. Wait: Our build.sh will automatically create the tables during the redeployment process.
